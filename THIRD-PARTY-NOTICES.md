# Third-party notices

Tec-Tac Core UI includes the packages below. Each one is listed with its exact version, its licence, its copyright holder and
where its licence text lives in the build.

| Package | Version | Licence | Copyright holder | Licence text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `@xterm/xterm` (xterm.js) | 5.5.0 | MIT | The xterm.js authors | `public/licenses/xterm-LICENSE.txt`, served at `/tec-tac/licenses/xterm-LICENSE.txt` |
| `@xterm/addon-fit` | 0.10.0 | MIT | The xterm.js authors | `public/licenses/xterm-LICENSE.txt` (the same file) |

xterm.js draws the terminal in the module windows (see `docs/module-terminal.md`). Its licence text is copied from each
package's own `LICENSE` file, word for word, into `public/licenses/xterm-LICENSE.txt`. The file ships in the signed build.
